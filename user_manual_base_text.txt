I want to make a user manual which will explain users how to operate our website and what they can do.

Approver : 

1. On the home page, the approver will see a snapshot of approval activity (total, pending, approved, and rejected RACMs) and links to the Approval Dashboard and Company Details. RACMs are assigned by company admin or coordinator based on assignment scope — unit level, unit + business process level, or RACM-specific assignment. When scopes overlap, the more specific assignment takes precedence.

2. On the Approval Dashboard, the approver will see all RACMs which are submitted by process owner. The dashboard includes filters for approval status, business process, financial year, unit (when more than one unit is in scope), conclusion, and control number search. By default, pending RACMs are shown. Clicking a RACM opens it in a new window with all important details listed on top.

3. Each RACM has the same major sections as the process owner view: (1) Process and Risk, (2) Control Details, and (3) Assertions (may not exists in some). The approver can review evidence/documents uploaded by the process owner. Audit logs and RACM history are also available from the RACM page.

4. For RACMs with status "sent for approval", the approver must either Approve or Reject the RACM.

(1) Approve — The approver must fill in Design and Implementation details: procedures taken to evaluate the control, and conclusion (Effective / Not Effective / Accepted under deviation). If the conclusion is Not Effective, a description of the deficiency is required. Reason for approval is optional.

(2) Reject — The approver must provide a reason for rejection. The process owner will need to upload at least one new document and resubmit the RACM.

5. After the initial approval decision, there are three main outcomes :

(1) RACM Rejected — Process owner must resubmit with at least one new document.

(2) RACM Approved - Not Effective — Process owner (or coordinator) must submit a deficiency response: either a Mitigation Plan or Compensatory RACM. The approver will see the submitted response on the RACM page and must review it.

(3) RACM Approved - Effective / Accepted under Deviation — No further action is required.

6. Deficiency response review — When a deficiency response is submitted, the dashboard shows an "Action Required" alert. On the RACM page, the approver can approve the response as Effective or Accepted under deviation, or reject it. Rejection requires a comment. If rejected, the RACM remains Not Effective and another response is required from the process owner.

7. Types of deficiency responses (for reference when reviewing):

(1) Mitigation Plan — Includes explanation, concerned person, and due date (today or a future date).

(2) Compensatory RACM — Includes explanation and at least one uploaded document.
