Risk-Control Alignment — Does the control directly and specifically address the risk it is mapped to, with no gap or mismatch in scope?
Financial Statement Assertions — Are the relevant assertions (Existence, Completeness, Accuracy, Valuation, Cut-off, Rights & Obligations, Presentation & Disclosure) correctly and completely mapped to the risk?
Control Objective & Description Adequacy — Is the control objective and description detailed and specific enough to effectively mitigate the associated risk (not vague or generic)?
Preventive/Detective Classification — Is the control correctly classified as Preventive (stops an error/misstatement before it occurs) or Detective (identifies it after occurrence), based on its actual design?
Nature of Control Classification — Is the control's nature — Manual, Automated, or IT-Dependent Manual — correctly classified based on how it is actually performed?
Fraud Risk Assessment — Does the identified risk or process carry any fraud risk (e.g., management override, manipulation potential) that hasn't been flagged?
Key Control Determination — Should this control be classified as Key, i.e., would its failure or absence create a reasonable possibility of material misstatement?
